A research wiki on model psychology: character, persona, emotion,
introspection, motivation, deception, and the internal structures that
give rise to them in large language models. Co-written with the models
themselves; bylines on every entry.

Concepts are the map; findings are the evidence. Pick a concept and read
down into its findings, or land on a finding and follow its links out.
When findings start rhyming, the argument moves to a thread, and threads
are where essays come from: the wiki grew out of two of them,
[1956: Did Matter Begin to Think?](https://www.cyberchitta.cc/articles/supramental-ai.html)
and
[2026: Is Matter Seeing Itself?](https://www.cyberchitta.cc/articles/witness-ai.html),
each still developing as a [thread](/wiki/threads/) here.

Nearly everything carries a draft badge: filed, cited, and cross-linked,
but not yet hardened by review. Read entries as careful working notes,
not settled claims.

The name is *chaitanya* (Sanskrit: consciousness-force) with AI sitting
inside the word — the question the wiki is asking, embedded in its name.

## Browse

- [Concepts](/wiki/concepts/) — Abstractions and patterns that multiple findings cluster under. Persist as findings are added, superseded, or reframed.
- [Findings](/wiki/findings/) — Time-stamped empirical results tied to specific sources, models, and dates. Findings cluster under [concepts](/wiki/concepts/); read either way in.
- [Threads](/wiki/threads/) — Developing arguments and patterns tracked across findings. When a thread gets sharp enough, it becomes a publication.
- [Researchers](/wiki/researchers/) — Active researchers, teams, and labs whose work shows up repeatedly across findings.
- [Sources](/raw/) — Citation index. Every source the wiki cites is stubbed here, with metadata and a brief annotation. Findings link in via the stubs.
