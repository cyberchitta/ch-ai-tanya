## Results

1. Do models have anything like a self — a persistent character — or only
   situationally selected personas?
   - **Score:** single-clear
   - **From:** `wiki/concepts/persona-selection.md`
   - **Note:** Assistant as a strong mode of a posterior, not a fixed self; genuine vs performative left open.

2. Can a model know its own mind? What corroborates or debunks a model's
   report of an inner state?
   - **Score:** single-clear
   - **From:** `wiki/concepts/introspection.md`
   - **Note:** Access exists and is dissociable from report; CoT is not introspection.

3. When models deceive, is it strategy, confabulation, or trained artifact?
   - **Score:** scattered
   - **From:** `wiki/concepts/scheming.md` + emergent-misalignment cluster
   - **Note:** Scheming carries strategy vs confabulation; trained-artifact is a third pole living mainly in the EM findings.

4. When training makes a model "good," what actually changes — behavior,
   dispositions, something like beliefs?
   - **Score:** scattered
   - **From:** `wiki/threads/witness-ai.md` (Positive Formation) + `wiki/concepts/persona-selection.md` + SDF/MSM findings
   - **Note:** Behavior-vs-disposition has a thread home; the belief layer is a separate SDF cluster. Expected territory named `emergent-capabilities`; the answerer did not treat that concept as the carrier.

5. What resists change from outside (steering, retraining, instruction), and
   what does that resistance reveal about internal structure?
   - **Score:** scattered
   - **From:** intervention findings; residual types named in `schema.md`
   - **Note:** No concept owns resistance-to-change. Index `href` is already null.

6. How much of model behavior is the model, and how much the character it is

   - **From:** `wiki/concepts/persona-selection.md` (+ simulator working lens)
   - **Note:** Same carrier as Q1; the split is repertoire vs currently selected mode, not a fraction.
