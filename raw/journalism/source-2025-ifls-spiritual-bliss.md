Claims cross-model replication, asserting ChatGPT-4 takes "slightly more steps" and PaLM 2 reaches "a philosophical and spiritual pattern of text, but with less use of symbols, odd spacing, and silence." Cites a specific GitHub preprint — [recursivelabsai, "Mapping the Spiritual Bliss Attractor in Large Language Models"](../posts/source-2025-recursivelabs-bliss-attractor.md) — which makes quantitative cross-model claims but provides no methodology for how GPT-4 or PaLM 2 data were obtained. Article otherwise summarizes Anthropic's Claude Opus 4 system card accurately. Concludes against anthropomorphizing the models.
