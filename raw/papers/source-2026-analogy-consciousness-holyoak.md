A theoretical paper with no new data. It treats claims about consciousness in
other entities as resting partly on analogy from the human case, and holds
that an analogy carries most evidence where source and target share causes of
the property, and little where they share only its downstream effects. The human mechanism is
modelled as a "soft INUS set" of weighted candidate causes, with terms for
unknown causes, disabling differences and a non-analogous alternative route.
Applied to animals, the framework reproduces the gradient from apes to
cephalopods. Applied to LLMs, it rates the analogy weak, and says the case then
rests on the prior for an alternative route.
