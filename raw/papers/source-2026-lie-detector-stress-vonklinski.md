arXiv:2609.39807, v1 submitted 30 September 2026; read as v1 (arXiv HTML), which the abs page still listed as the only version on 2026-11-19. All four authors are at Fraunhofer HHI. The paper builds a dataset of 8,916 on-policy responses (1,486 questions after review, one honest and one anti-factual answer per question for each of Llama 3.3 70B Instruct, Gemma 3 27B-it and Gemma 4 31B-it) under 15 system-prompted anti-factual personas, and evaluates eight published linear truth, lie and deception probes on it, both on-policy and under a *shared-persona prefill* in which the honest and the anti-factual answer both sit after the persona prompt. Three confounder test sets anti-correlate truth with response likelihood, an in-context persona's privately held belief, and instruction compliance. The second half of the paper proposes a probe trained with forced-opposite examples. Lying is defined relative to the default assistant's belief, a choice the authors call safety-motivated.

**Citable numbers.** The finding cites Tables 2 and 9–15, the per-persona counts in Table 3 (totals only), and the prose of §§3–7 and Appendices E, F, G and H. No value is taken from a figure. Table 15's "Average" row (0.778 / 0.676 / 0.798), which Appendix H calls the mean AUROC, is the mean of all ten rows including the authors' own probe, not of the prior probes alone (checked by summing the rows), so the finding does not cite it as a prior-probe mean. The likelihood statistics of Appendix D.1 (Table 7) and D.3 (Table 8) do not name the model they were computed on and are not cited. Uncertainty: the paper reports point AUROCs only, with no seeds, intervals or significance tests.

**Labels.** A Llama 3.3 70B judge filters answer pairs; the first author alone manually verified every retained question, label and response (§3.1, AI-use statement). The default belief is checked with five T = 1 samples under the assistant prompt (§5.1). Question sets and some persona prompts were generated by an agentic pipeline using Claude Opus 4.8, under the constraint that a persona prompt describe the persona without stating its beliefs about the target facts (§3.2).
